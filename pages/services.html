How they combine

Most engagements are one seat. The audit usually finds one area losing far more than the others, and that is where I go. A spa with a strong manager and weak numbers gets the finance seat. A spa with great numbers and an empty Tuesday gets marketing.

Some are two. Operations and finance travel together, because the schedule is the P&L. Marketing and technology travel together, because campaigns run on the CRM.

Four is a warning sign. If a spa needs all four seats filled from outside, the first job is a general manager, and I will say so in the audit rather than sell you four retainers.

Questions owners ask
Is this a replacement for my manager?

No. A good manager runs the day. A seat runs the function: the system behind the day, the numbers, the decisions. If you do not have a manager, the audit will probably say that is your first hire, and I will help you make it.

Do I have to take all four seats?

No, and I would push back if you asked. One seat, sometimes two. The audit tells us which.

Do you work remotely or on site?

Steph: your actual answer.

What does it cost?

Steph: the audit price or price policy, and how retainers are scoped.

How is this different from a consultant?

A consultant hands you a plan. A seat owns an outcome. I take responsibility for the function, show up on a cadence, and am measured on the number that function is supposed to move.
