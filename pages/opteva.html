Not a generic AI tool. Each client carries a stored voice profile covering tone, audience, the phrases they use and the ones they refuse. Every generation runs through it, so the writing sounds like that business instead of like the category.

I built it because I had been the owner sending the draft back with "this isn't really us" and no notes. That is the problem it solves, and it is why it works differently.

For the fractional practice, Opteva is a tool the marketing and technology seats can deploy when a spa needs consistent social and nobody on staff has the hours. It is not a requirement of any engagement.
