Who this is for

You are the COO, the CFO, the CMO and the CTO. You are also the injector.

A single-location spa doing one to five million a year is a real business with no executive team. The owner carries every seat between appointments, and the business grows exactly as fast as that owner's spare hours. If any of these sound familiar, that is the gap.

Revenue is up and take-home is flat.

More appointments, more product, more staff, same money at the end of the month. Nobody is looking at margin by service, and the prices were set three years ago.

The team runs on your memory.

Schedules, rebooking, inventory, how the front desk answers the phone. It works because you are there. The week you are not, it slips.

The numbers arrive late, if at all.

The bookkeeper closes the month six weeks after it ends. Marketing spend is a feeling. The booking system, the CRM and the phones do not talk to each other.

One operator, four seats

Pick the seat that is bleeding. I have sat in all four.

Most owners do not need four executives. They need one person who understands how the four connect, and the discipline to fix the one that matters first. The audit tells us which. The seat pages tell you what each one covers.
