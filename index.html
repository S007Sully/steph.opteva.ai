About Me

Most business problems are not skill problems.

I ran a med spa for over two decades. I was doing the treatments, managing the team, handling the marketing and figuring out why clients were not rebooking. I lived every pain point operators talk about.

What I learned after 26 years is that most of it is not a skill problem. It is a systems problem. You are good at what you do. You are just missing the infrastructure that makes it visible, consistent and repeatable when you are busy.

So I spent a year studying AI, then built Opteva around the gaps I already knew existed because I had spent half my life inside them. I ranked my own company to the top of Google without an agency. That is when I knew this was worth building properly.

I have been the owner sending the draft back with "this isn't really us" and no notes. That is the problem I built around.

What I Built

After 26 years running a med spa and a year deep in AI systems, I built Opteva. A content system for agencies and operators who cannot keep social consistent on their own, because the person who knows how the business actually sounds is the one person without a spare hour.

Not a generic AI tool. Each client carries a stored voice profile covering tone, audience, the phrases they use and the ones they refuse. Every generation runs through it, so the writing sounds like that business instead of like the category.

I built it because I needed it first. That is the whole reason it works differently.
